Why Dogs are the Best

A page about why dogs are so awesome

Dogs are the oldest domesticated animalfossil records show that dogs have been domesticated from the Gray Wold

Dogs have been domesticated for around 30,000 years. They make great pets with for a family, as well as help us with our work.

Three facts about dogs

  • A dog's smell is 10,000 times more powerful than a human's.
  • Dogs live to around 11 years old.
  • Dogs have three eyelids instead of the human two.
